# Showcase artwork provenance

These are original, unofficial fan interpretations. They are not official screenshots or game assets.
Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-07; incorporated into actual HTML/CSS/Canvas themes. The README previews are browser screenshots of those themes, not painted mockups.

## Sources of the thematic brief

- Three-body / 三体: Liu Cixin's trilogy; [publisher series page](https://us.macmillan.com/series/thethreebodyproblemseries).
- Genshin Impact / 原神, Sumeru: [official world introduction](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/map) and [Version 3.0 announcement](https://www.hoyoverse.com/en-us/news/102755).

The observatory is a thematic interpretation, not a depiction of a specific canon building. Its three-star choreography is a visual model, not an astronomical simulation. The Sumeru garden and interactions interpret the themes of knowledge, nature and elemental reactions; they do not reproduce game rules or a specific in-game location.
